## 2. Purpose and marketplace model

2.1 Everneed operates, or intends to operate, a digital commerce marketplace that helps customers discover participating businesses and place orders with sellers. Everneed's intended positioning is **“A Digital Commerce Ecosystem.”**

2.2 The marketplace may provide digital storefronts, product discovery, order-management tools, payment-provider integrations, customer support coordination and, where separately offered and accepted, fulfilment or delivery coordination.

2.3 The Seller lists and supplies its own goods. Unless a particular transaction is expressly documented otherwise, the Seller—not Everneed—is the supplier and seller of those goods to the customer. The Seller remains responsible for product legality, description, quality, safety, stock, packaging before handover, and obligations that applicable law places on the supplier.

2.4 Everneed's role must be understood from the actual transaction, representations, payment arrangements and services performed. Nothing in this Agreement removes a legal responsibility that applies to Everneed because of its own conduct or services.

2.5 The marketplace may support:
- **Single-seller orders:** an order containing goods from one Seller, potentially delivered directly by that Seller or through an agreed hub-assisted route.
- **Multi-seller orders:** one customer-facing parent order containing separate seller-specific packages or sub-orders. Eligible packages may be received at an Everneed-designated hub, checked, consolidated and dispatched together where operationally safe and practical. Separate parcels may be used where required.

2.6 The hub and delivery model is a planned operating model. A hub, route, carrier, service area or service level is available only where Everneed has actually established it and expressly enabled it for the relevant order. This Agreement does not represent that a physical hub, fleet, carrier contract or delivery coverage already exists.

## 3. Definitions

In this Agreement:

- **Applicable Law** means Nigerian law and any other law that applies to the relevant Party, product, service, transaction or processing activity.
- **Business Day** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in the place where the relevant obligation is to be performed, unless the applicable schedule states otherwise.
- **Customer** means a person who uses the marketplace to purchase or seek to purchase goods.
- **Customer Order** means the customer-facing order record, which may contain one or more Seller Packages.
- **Seller Package** means the portion of a Customer Order containing goods supplied by one Seller.
- **Seller Content** means the Seller's store name, logo, product text, photographs, prices, stock information, policies and other materials supplied by or on behalf of the Seller.
- **Qualifying Completed Sale** has the meaning in clause 11 and Schedule 2.
- **Payment Provider** means a bank, payment service provider or other appropriately authorised provider selected for the relevant payment flow.
- **Hub Service** means a separately described receiving, inspection, sorting, temporary custody, consolidation, packaging or dispatch service actually made available by Everneed or its contracted operator.
- **Delivery Partner** means a carrier, courier, rider or logistics provider engaged for a particular delivery route.
- **Seller Account Statement** means the transaction-level record of sales, approved deductions, refunds, reversals and amounts payable to the Seller.

## 4. Seller onboarding, identity and eligibility

4.1 The Seller must provide accurate and current information reasonably required to:
- identify the Seller and, where applicable, its authorised representatives and beneficial owners;
- establish contact and store details;
- assess the Seller's authority to sell the listed goods;
- meet applicable legal, tax, safety, payment-provider and fraud-prevention requirements; and
- enable order, settlement and customer-support operations.

4.2 Everneed must request information proportionate to the Seller's risk and legal obligations. It should not demand irrelevant personal or business information merely as a condition of basic participation.

4.3 The Seller must promptly notify Everneed of material changes to its legal name, ownership, address, contact details, bank or approved settlement details, licences, product authorisations or authority to act.

4.4 Everneed may request additional evidence where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, product safety, identity mismatch, legal compliance or a payment-provider requirement. The request and reason should be explained where doing so is lawful and does not compromise an investigation.

4.5 Approval to join the marketplace is not a government licence, product certification, guarantee of product quality or endorsement of every Seller claim. Any verification badge must be based on a documented and accurately described verification standard.

4.6 The Seller must not create duplicate, misleading or impersonating stores, misrepresent its identity, use another person's payment details without authority, or allow an unauthorised person to control its Seller Account.

## 5. Seller obligations and store management

The Seller agrees to:

a. operate its store honestly and in compliance with Applicable Law;  
b. maintain accurate business/contact information and respond to reasonable platform requests;  
c. keep store descriptions, opening status, service availability and policies current;  
d. maintain secure access credentials and promptly report suspected account compromise;  
e. use the marketplace only for lawful commerce and not manipulate reviews, orders, rankings, referrals or transactions;  
f. treat customers, Everneed staff and delivery personnel respectfully;  
g. cooperate with reasonable investigations into orders, complaints, safety issues, fraud and payment disputes;  
h. maintain records needed to support sales, fulfilment, tax and product traceability; and  
i. avoid conduct likely to cause foreseeable harm to customers, other sellers or the integrity of the marketplace.

The Seller remains an independent business. This Agreement does not, by itself, create employment, partnership, franchise, joint venture or a general agency relationship. Any specific agency or authority must be expressly documented and legally reviewed.

## 6. Product listings, stock and pricing

6.1 The Seller is responsible for ensuring that every listing is truthful, complete, current and not misleading.

6.2 Listings must disclose material product information, including as relevant:
- product identity, key features, materials, dimensions, compatibility and condition;
- whether goods are new, used, refurbished, reconditioned or otherwise not new;
- model, variant, size, colour, quantity and included accessories;
- safety warnings, instructions, warranty or guarantee information;
- relevant registration, certification, labelling or sector-specific approvals;
- accurate photographs that represent the actual product; and
- any material limitation, exclusion or delivery restriction.

6.3 The Seller must not list counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, prohibited, restricted or unlawfully marketed goods. The Seller must comply with applicable NAFDAC, SON, FCCPC, intellectual-property, import, labelling and sector-specific requirements where relevant. Everneed may maintain a restricted/prohibited goods schedule and update it when reasonably necessary for safety or legal compliance.

6.4 The Seller controls its product prices and stock, subject to Applicable Law and the requirement not to mislead customers. The Seller must update stock promptly and must not accept orders for goods it cannot reasonably supply.

6.5 The Seller must not change a price after a customer has placed an order except through a process that obtains the customer's informed agreement before any additional amount is charged.

6.6 The Seller must honour a confirmed order at the disclosed price unless cancellation or adjustment is permitted by law and the applicable order rules.

6.7 Everneed may reject, hide, pause or remove a listing where it reasonably suspects inaccurate information, a legal or safety issue, intellectual-property infringement, fraud, or a breach of this Agreement. Except where immediate action is reasonably required, Everneed should tell the Seller the reason and provide a reasonable opportunity to correct the issue.

## 7. Intellectual property and Seller Content

7.1 The Seller retains ownership of Seller Content and any intellectual-property rights it owns or is authorised to use.

7.2 The Seller grants the Platform Operator a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence for the term of this Agreement to host, reproduce, format, display, distribute and communicate Seller Content solely as reasonably necessary to:
- operate and promote the Seller's store and listings on Everneed;
- process, fulfil and support Customer Orders;
- provide order information to the relevant customer, Payment Provider, Hub Service operator or Delivery Partner;
- advertise the marketplace and participating stores using accurate Seller Content; and
- maintain lawful records, backups, security and dispute evidence.

7.3 Everneed must not sell or license Seller Content for unrelated commercial use without separate permission. The licence does not transfer ownership to Everneed.

7.4 The Seller warrants that it owns or has sufficient rights and permissions for the Seller Content and that its use as authorised by this Agreement will not knowingly infringe another person's rights.

7.5 The Seller must promptly notify Everneed of a credible intellectual-property complaint. Everneed may temporarily restrict disputed content while it reviews a complaint, gives the Seller an opportunity to respond where appropriate, and follows Applicable Law.

7.6 Each Party retains its pre-existing intellectual property, software, brands, processes and materials. No ownership of Everneed's platform, code, brand or systems transfers to the Seller.

## 8. Orders, acceptance and preparation

8.1 A product listing or basket does not by itself guarantee stock or create a completed sale. The customer-facing terms and checkout must explain when an order request is submitted, when payment is authorised or confirmed, and when the Seller accepts the Seller Package.

8.2 Everneed must communicate the order status and the Seller's required action through the available order channel. The Seller must monitor that channel and keep its contact details current.

8.3 The Seller must accept or reject an order within the response period stated in Schedule 3. If no response period has been approved, the parties must not imply a service-level guarantee; the order may be cancelled under the customer-facing terms after reasonable notice.

8.4 After accepting a Seller Package, the Seller must:
- confirm stock and order details;
- prepare the correct goods and quantities;
- check goods for visible damage and completeness;
- package goods safely for the agreed route;
- label the package with the order/package reference and any handling instructions;
- meet the agreed ready-for-collection or hub-drop-off time; and
- update the order status truthfully.

8.5 The Seller must not substitute a product, variant, quantity or material specification without the customer's informed approval through the approved process.

8.6 If the Seller cannot fulfil an accepted order, it must notify Everneed promptly with the reason and affected items. Everneed will coordinate customer communication and the appropriate cancellation/refund process. A seller-caused cancellation may affect performance metrics and may result in recovery of a direct, evidenced cost only where Schedule 2 expressly permits it, the Seller caused the cost, and the deduction is lawful and fairly disputed.

8.7 Everneed must not impose a penalty merely because a Seller rejects an order before acceptance, where rejection is permitted under the order rules and is not abusive or systematically misleading.

## 9. Fulfilment, hub services and delivery

9.1 The fulfilment route for each order must be shown or otherwise clearly communicated before the customer commits to any route-specific charge.

9.2 **Single-seller direct delivery.** Where enabled and agreed, the Seller may deliver directly to the customer. The Seller is responsible for the delivery service it provides, including accurate dispatch information, safe handling, customer communication and proof of delivery.

9.3 **Single-seller hub-assisted delivery.** Where the Seller or Everneed selects an available hub-assisted service, the Seller must prepare and hand over the package at the agreed location and time. The hub and onward delivery responsibilities must be set out in the relevant service schedule or order instructions.

9.4 **Multi-seller consolidation.** A multi-seller Customer Order may contain separate Seller Packages. Each Seller remains responsible for its own goods and for preparing its package correctly. Packages may be sent or dropped at a designated hub, checked against package references, temporarily held, consolidated and dispatched together where safe and practical. More than one parcel may be required because of size, fragility, incompatibility, handling requirements or operational constraints.

9.5 **Package handover and custody.** Each handover must be recorded with, as appropriate, package ID, order reference, date/time, receiving party, package count and visible condition. The Seller remains responsible for loss or damage occurring before the package is received and recorded by the agreed recipient, except to the extent caused by another party. After recorded acceptance, the party exercising custody is responsible for reasonable care while the package is in its control, subject to Applicable Law, the service contract and proven cause of loss or damage.

9.6 A hub receipt or visual check is not a certification of hidden product quality, authenticity, legal compliance or the accuracy of every product claim. It does not remove the Seller's responsibility for defective, unsafe, counterfeit, misdescribed or non-conforming goods.

9.7 Everneed must not charge the Seller for a Hub Service, collection or delivery service that the Seller did not select or agree to, except where the service is an expressly disclosed and accepted part of a specific order arrangement or a lawful, evidenced remedy for a Seller-caused breach.

9.8 The Seller must not be charged for a failure caused solely by Everneed, its hub operator or a carrier selected and controlled by Everneed, unless the Seller caused or materially contributed to the failure.

9.9 Delivery estimates are estimates, not guarantees, unless a written service schedule expressly provides a guaranteed service and states its remedy. Everneed must not advertise a route or delivery time that has not been operationally validated.

9.10 The Seller must cooperate with delivery investigations, including providing preparation records, package details, handover evidence and relevant communications.

## 10. Customer service, returns and complaints

10.1 Everneed may operate a coordinated customer-facing support process. The Seller must respond promptly to reasonable requests concerning its goods, order preparation, product information, warranty, return eligibility and alleged defects.

10.2 The Parties must follow Applicable Law and the customer-facing returns/refunds policy in force for the transaction. That policy must not reduce statutory customer rights.

10.3 The Seller is responsible for a remedy to the extent the issue is attributable to the Seller's proven act or omission, including:
- wrong, missing or materially misdescribed goods;
- defective or unsafe goods for which the Seller is legally responsible;
- failure to disclose material product condition or limitations;
- seller-caused cancellation or failure to prepare an accepted order; or
- breach of an express Seller warranty.

10.4 Everneed is responsible for a remedy to the extent the issue is attributable to Everneed's own proven act or omission, including its own platform error, staff handling or a Hub Service it controls. A Delivery Partner's responsibility must be assessed under the relevant carrier arrangement and Applicable Law. The Seller must not automatically bear a loss caused solely by Everneed or its selected provider.

10.5 Where responsibility is disputed, Everneed must preserve relevant evidence, explain the provisional basis for its decision, allow the affected Party to provide information, and give a route for review. Urgent safety or legal action may be taken while the review continues.

10.6 Refunds must be processed through the applicable approved payment method and provider workflow. A refund must not be described as completed until the provider confirms completion. Any amount due from the Seller must be supported by the transaction record, contractual basis and applicable law.

10.7 Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits a customer's non-excludable rights under the FCCPA or other Applicable Law. The Seller must not direct a customer to waive a statutory right as a condition of complaint handling.

## 11. Commission, fees and commercial terms

11.1 Everneed's commercial approach is intended to be transparent and proportionate. No commission rate, subscription, joining fee, advertising charge, hub fee or other Seller charge is created merely by this Agreement.

11.2 The Seller's approved commission rate, calculation base, applicable taxes, earning event and any optional service charges must be completed in **Schedule 2** and accepted by the Seller before the relevant charge applies.

11.3 Unless Schedule 2 expressly states otherwise and has been legally reviewed, commission may be charged only on a **Qualifying Completed Sale**. The intended default definition is a Seller Package for which:
- payment has been confirmed through the approved provider process;
- the goods have been delivered, with valid delivery evidence;
- the transaction has not been cancelled, fully refunded, reversed or identified as fraudulent; and
- any applicable return or statutory remedy has been accounted for.

The exact treatment of partial refunds, chargebacks, partial delivery, customer acceptance and the return period must be specified in Schedule 2 before launch. An unresolved complaint or payment dispute must not be treated as an undisputed completed sale for deduction purposes.

11.4 No commission is payable on a cancelled, unpaid, failed, fully refunded or reversed transaction. Where a later partial refund or chargeback affects a previously calculated commission, the adjustment must be itemised and explained.

11.5 No compulsory seller joining fee or basic-store subscription applies under this draft. Any future subscription or paid feature must be optional, separately described, priced in advance and accepted by the Seller without misleading the Seller about access to ordinary marketplace participation.

11.6 Advertising, sponsored placement and promotions are optional. Paid placement must be labelled. The Seller decides whether to fund a discount or promotion, and the funding split must be agreed in writing before the promotion begins. Everneed must not secretly suppress ordinary listings to compel payment for visibility.

11.7 A Seller must not be charged for an unselected service. Delivery and hub fees must identify the service, route, charging party, calculation basis and any applicable tax before acceptance.

11.8 Every deduction from a Seller's proceeds must have a clear contractual or legal basis, a transaction reference, calculation and description. Everneed must not impose arbitrary penalties, hidden charges or indefinite holds.

11.9 No change to a commission rate, fee, calculation basis or mandatory commercial term applies retrospectively. A change requires advance notice and the Seller's affirmative acceptance where it materially increases the Seller's obligations. If the Seller does not accept, the Seller may stop accepting new orders and terminate under clause 17, subject to open orders and legal obligations.

## 12. Payments, settlement and seller records

12.1 Customer payments must be processed through a Payment Provider and transaction structure that has been approved for the actual marketplace model and is permitted by Applicable Law and the provider's contract.

12.2 This Agreement does not authorise Everneed to provide banking, deposit-taking, payment service, wallet, escrow or money transmission services. Everneed must not hold or use Seller funds as its own operating cash. The legal and operational structure for collection, split settlement, deductions, refunds and payouts must be approved with the Payment Provider and qualified Nigerian payments counsel before live transactions.

12.3 The Seller must provide accurate settlement details through the approved secure process. Everneed must not request card PINs, one-time passwords, full card credentials or unrelated banking secrets.

12.4 The Seller Account Statement should show, where applicable:
- order and Seller Package references;
- gross item value and customer-paid amounts attributable to the Seller;
- approved discounts and who funded them;
- commission and its calculation;
- optional services actually used;
- taxes or statutory deductions, with their basis;
- refunds, reversals and chargebacks;
- adjustments and disputes; and
- net amount due and settlement status.

12.5 The settlement timetable, provider dependencies, minimum payout threshold if any, fees, reserve rules if any, and permitted hold reasons must be completed in Schedule 2. No indefinite or unexplained hold is permitted under this draft.

12.6 A hold may be applied only where reasonably necessary for a documented legal requirement, provider instruction, suspected fraud, security incident, genuine transaction dispute, refund exposure or other basis stated in the approved schedule. Everneed must disclose the reason and review the hold periodically, unless prohibited by law or an authority.

12.7 The Seller may challenge a statement or deduction by giving the transaction reference and reasons. Everneed must investigate against available records and correct verified errors within a reasonable period. Undisputed amounts should not be withheld merely because another amount is disputed, where the payment structure and law permit separate settlement.

12.8 The Seller is responsible for its own tax obligations on its business income and supplies, subject to Applicable Law. Everneed may make or report deductions only where legally required or expressly agreed and lawful. The Parties must obtain tax advice on invoices, VAT, withholding and reporting before the marketplace goes live.

## 13. Data protection and confidentiality

13.1 Each Party must comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable privacy and cybersecurity laws to the extent they apply to its activities.

13.2 The Parties' roles as independent controllers, joint controllers or controller/processor must be assessed from the actual purposes and means of processing. This Agreement does not assume that every Seller or Everneed has the same role for every data activity.

13.3 The Seller may use customer personal data received through Everneed only to fulfil the relevant order, provide legally required after-sales support, manage a lawful return/warranty issue and meet a legal obligation. The Seller must not:
- add customers to unrelated marketing lists without a valid lawful basis and required consent;
- sell, rent, disclose or reuse customer information for unrelated purposes;
- contact customers outside the order purpose in a harassing or misleading way;
- retain personal data longer than necessary; or
- share credentials or order data with unauthorised persons.

13.4 The Seller must use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, restrict staff access, protect devices and accounts, and promptly notify Everneed of any suspected loss, unauthorised access, disclosure or misuse of marketplace personal data. The notice should be given without undue delay and include known facts, affected data and containment steps. The Parties must cooperate on assessment, mitigation and any legally required notification.

13.5 Everneed must provide a privacy notice explaining its own processing, sharing, retention, rights and contact arrangements. Where a separate data-processing agreement is legally required, the Parties must execute it before the relevant processing begins.

13.6 Confidential Information includes non-public commercial, operational, technical, security, financial and personal information disclosed by one Party to the other. The receiving Party must use it only for this Agreement, protect it reasonably, and disclose it only to authorised personnel or where legally required.

13.7 Confidentiality does not prevent disclosure of information that is public without breach, already lawfully known, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source. A Party required by law to disclose information should, where lawful, give advance notice and disclose only what is required.

13.8 On termination, each Party must return, delete or securely retain information as required by law, legitimate recordkeeping needs and the applicable privacy notice. Personal data must not be retained indefinitely merely because it was once associated with an order.

## 14. Platform access, security and service changes

14.1 Everneed may maintain, update, suspend or improve platform features for security, maintenance, legal compliance and product development. It should give reasonable notice of material planned interruptions where practicable.

14.2 Everneed does not guarantee uninterrupted availability, error-free operation or a particular level of traffic, ranking, sales or income. This does not excuse Everneed from responsibility for its own contractual obligations or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.

14.3 The Seller must not probe, disrupt, reverse engineer, scrape, overload, bypass security controls, introduce malicious code or access another Seller's information without authority.

14.4 The Seller must report suspected security incidents promptly and cooperate with reasonable containment steps.

14.5 Everneed may change platform features, interfaces and technical processes. A change that materially alters the Seller's commercial obligations, fees, data rights or fulfilment responsibilities must follow the notice and acceptance process in clause 11.9.

## 15. Fair enforcement, restrictions and appeals

15.1 Everneed may investigate suspected breaches and apply proportionate measures, including a request for correction, listing restriction, order pause, temporary account restriction or termination.

15.2 Except where immediate action is reasonably necessary to protect a person, prevent fraud, comply with law or authority, protect payment/security systems, preserve evidence or prevent serious harm, Everneed should:
- notify the Seller of the material concern;
- provide sufficient detail to allow a meaningful response, subject to lawful confidentiality limits;
- allow a reasonable opportunity to correct or respond;
- consider the Seller's evidence; and
- communicate the decision and available review route.

15.3 The Seller may appeal a material restriction or termination by using the contact route in Schedule 1. A person not responsible for the original decision should review the appeal where reasonably practicable.

15.4 Everneed must not use suspension as a device to confiscate Seller funds or avoid paying undisputed amounts. Any lawful hold must meet clause 12.6 and be reviewed.

15.5 Everneed may immediately remove or restrict a product where it reasonably suspects that the product is illegal, unsafe, counterfeit, recalled or otherwise presents urgent risk. The Seller must cooperate with product withdrawal, customer notification and regulator requirements.

## 16. Representations, warranties and responsibility

16.1 Each Party represents that it has the legal capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement and perform its obligations.

16.2 The Seller represents, on an ongoing basis, that:
- it is accurately identified and authorised to sell the listed goods;
- Seller Content is accurate and does not knowingly infringe third-party rights;
- goods are lawfully sourced, genuine where represented as genuine, safe and as described;
- it holds licences, registrations and approvals required for its products and business;
- it will comply with applicable consumer, product safety, tax, privacy and trade requirements; and
- it will not knowingly engage in fraud, deceptive marketing or unlawful discrimination.

16.3 Everneed represents that it will perform the platform and any separately accepted services with reasonable care and in accordance with the express terms applicable to those services.

16.4 Each Party is responsible for loss to the extent caused by its own breach, negligence, fraud, wilful misconduct or other legally attributable act or omission.

16.5 Any indemnity must be limited to third-party claims, direct loss categories, causation, mitigation and procedures expressly agreed by the Parties and approved by Nigerian counsel. No indemnity may require a Party to compensate another for that other Party's own fraud, wilful misconduct or liability that cannot lawfully be transferred.

16.6 Neither Party may exclude or limit liability where Applicable Law prohibits the exclusion or limitation, including any non-excludable consumer, product-safety, fraud, data-protection or other statutory responsibility.

16.7 Any agreed liability cap, excluded loss category, insurance requirement or claims procedure must be completed in Schedule 4 after legal and commercial review. No monetary cap is created by this draft.

## 17. Term, suspension and termination

17.1 This Agreement begins on the effective date and continues until terminated under this clause.

17.2 Either Party may terminate for convenience by giving the notice period in Schedule 4. If no period has been completed, the Parties must agree one before execution.

17.3 A Party may terminate for a material breach if the other Party fails to remedy it within a reasonable written cure period, except where the breach cannot be remedied or immediate termination is reasonably necessary.

17.4 Everneed may suspend or terminate immediately where reasonably necessary because of:
- credible evidence of fraud, identity theft or serious marketplace abuse;
- illegal, dangerous, counterfeit or recalled goods;
- a binding legal or regulatory direction;
- a serious security or privacy threat;
- material risk to customers or other sellers; or
- repeated material breach after prior notice.

Where legally and operationally safe, Everneed should provide reasons and an appeal route.

17.5 On termination:
- the Seller must stop accepting new orders through the marketplace;
- the Parties must cooperate to complete, cancel or otherwise resolve open orders fairly;
- customer complaints, returns, refunds and safety issues must be handled to conclusion;
- undisputed Seller amounts must be reconciled and paid through the approved provider structure, subject only to lawful and documented adjustments or holds;
- each Party must return or delete the other's Confidential Information subject to lawful retention; and
- clauses intended to survive, including payment reconciliation, privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, liability, dispute resolution and accrued rights, continue as necessary.

17.6 Termination does not erase a customer's statutory rights or either Party's accrued obligations.

## 18. Changes to this Agreement

18.1 Everneed may propose changes to platform policies, operational processes or this Agreement. The notice must identify the material change and its intended effective date.

18.2 A change that materially increases Seller fees, changes commission calculation, imposes a new mandatory service, changes risk allocation or materially reduces Seller rights requires the Seller's affirmative acceptance before it applies to new orders.

18.3 If the Seller rejects a material change, the Seller may stop taking new orders and terminate under clause 17, while the Parties resolve existing orders and balances under the version that applied to those transactions.

18.4 No policy page, dashboard notice or unilateral update may silently override an expressly agreed commercial schedule.

## 19. Records, audit and cooperation

19.1 Each Party must maintain accurate records reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement and Applicable Law.

19.2 Everneed may request relevant Seller records to investigate a specific order, customer complaint, product safety issue, suspected fraud, tax/legal request or payment dispute. Requests must be proportionate and limited to the matter being investigated.

19.3 Any audit or inspection right must be exercised on reasonable notice, during reasonable hours and in a manner that minimises disruption, except where urgent safety, fraud or legal circumstances require otherwise.

19.4 The Parties must preserve records subject to a legal hold, regulator request or active dispute, and securely dispose of them when retention is no longer justified.

## 20. Notices and communication

20.1 Formal notices must be sent to the physical or electronic addresses recorded in Schedule 1, using a method that provides reasonable evidence of delivery.

20.2 Routine order notices may be delivered through the Seller Account, email, SMS or another verified channel enabled by Everneed.

20.3 Each Party must keep its contact details current. A Party must not rely on an outdated address it failed to update.

## 21. Disputes and governing law

21.1 The Parties should first attempt to resolve a dispute through good-faith discussion between their designated contacts.

21.2 If unresolved within the period in Schedule 4, the dispute may be escalated to a senior representative of each Party for review.

21.3 Nothing in this clause prevents either Party or a customer from seeking urgent court relief, reporting suspected criminal conduct, contacting a competent regulator, or exercising a statutory right.

21.4 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, subject to any mandatory law that applies to the transaction.

21.5 The courts with competent jurisdiction in Nigeria may hear disputes unless the Parties later agree to a legally reviewed alternative dispute-resolution clause in Schedule 4. No compulsory arbitration venue, waiver of court access or restriction of statutory remedies is created by this draft.

## 22. General provisions

22.1 **Entire agreement:** This Agreement and its completed schedules form the agreement between the Parties about the subject matter and replace prior discussions on that subject, but do not override mandatory law or separately agreed payment-provider terms.

22.2 **Order of precedence:** If documents conflict, Applicable Law prevails. The signed commercial schedule prevails over a general platform policy for the specific commercial point it expressly covers. The customer-facing terms govern the customer's rights and must not be reduced by this Seller Agreement.

22.3 **Severability:** If a provision is invalid or unenforceable, it will be adjusted or severed only to the extent legally permitted; the remaining provisions continue.

22.4 **No waiver:** A failure or delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of that right.

22.5 **Assignment:** The Seller may not transfer this Agreement or its Seller Account without Everneed's prior written consent, which must not be unreasonably withheld for a legitimate business transfer where identity, compliance and customer obligations can be maintained. Everneed may transfer the Agreement to a successor operator only where lawful, with appropriate notice and protection of Seller rights.

22.6 **Third-party rights:** Except where Applicable Law provides otherwise or a clause expressly benefits a third party, this Agreement does not create enforceable rights for a person who is not a Party.

22.7 **Electronic acceptance:** Electronic signatures and click acceptance may be used only through a process that records the exact agreement version, identity/authority checks, affirmative action, timestamp and an accessible copy for each Party, after legal review.

### Commission and adjustment rules

1. No commission is due until the agreed Qualifying Completed Sale event occurs.
2. Cancelled, unpaid, failed, fully refunded or reversed sales do not attract commission.
3. Partial refunds require a proportional, itemised adjustment under the agreed calculation.
4. A chargeback or fraud claim must be investigated under the provider's rules and the evidence; it is not automatically proof of Seller fault.
5. Every debit must identify the order, reason, calculation and contractual basis.
6. No fee applies to a service the Seller did not select or agree to.
7. Any change requires the notice and acceptance process in clause 11.9.
