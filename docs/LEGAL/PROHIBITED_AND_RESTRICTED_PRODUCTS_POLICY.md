## Prohibited & Restricted Products and Listings Policy

**Initial market:** Nigeria

> **IMPORTANT — NOT READY FOR PUBLICATION.** This policy defines a proposed marketplace safety standard. It is not a complete statement of Nigerian law, legal advice, a regulator approval or proof that automated moderation and enforcement are operational. ## 1. Purpose

Everneed is being built as **A Digital Commerce Ecosystem**. A trustworthy marketplace must protect customers, legitimate sellers, rights holders and the wider public from unlawful, unsafe, deceptive or high-risk listings.

This policy establishes:
- Products and listings that must not be offered.
- Categories that require additional review or evidence before listing.
- Seller responsibilities for product legality, safety and authenticity.
- Everneed's review, removal, reporting and appeal principles.
- Launch controls needed before a category is enabled.

The policy applies to product listings, store content, promotional content, bundles, digital goods and services offered through Everneed, where relevant. A listing cannot avoid this policy by using coded words, misleading categories, altered photographs, hidden product details, private-message sales or a different description.

## 2. Core rules

1. Sellers may list only goods they are legally entitled to sell and that Everneed has enabled for the relevant category and location.
2. A product is not permitted merely because it is available elsewhere or a seller claims it is legal.
3. Required licences, registrations, authorisations, age restrictions, labelling and safety standards must be satisfied before a listing or sale is enabled.
4. Everneed may prohibit a product category even where some sales might be lawful, if the platform cannot responsibly verify or support it.
5. Sellers must provide accurate descriptions, images, ingredients, specifications, warnings, origin and compliance information relevant to the product.
6. Sellers must not make false health, safety, performance, origin, certification or endorsement claims.
7. Everneed may reject, restrict, pause or remove a listing where a credible risk arises, subject to proportionate review and applicable law.
8. No moderation decision should be represented as a formal government certification or safety approval.

## 3. Prohibited products and activity

The following categories are proposed as prohibited on Everneed, regardless of whether a narrow exception may exist elsewhere. Any exception requires written founder approval and qualified legal review before the category is enabled.

### 3.1 Illegal goods and services
- Goods whose possession, sale, import, export or distribution is unlawful.
- Stolen property, unlawfully obtained goods or property with credible evidence of theft.
- Products intended to facilitate fraud, identity theft, payment abuse, document forgery or other criminal conduct.
- Services that arrange, promote or facilitate unlawful activity.

### 3.2 Counterfeit, pirated and infringing goods
- Counterfeit branded products or replicas presented as genuine.
- Products bearing trademarks, logos or designs without authorisation where the listing infringes rights.
- Pirated software, games, films, music, books, course materials or other copyrighted works.
- Unauthorised activation keys, accounts, subscriptions or access credentials.
- Products or listings that misuse another person's images, designs, patents, trade secrets or other protected material.

A seller must be able to demonstrate legitimate sourcing or authorisation where a credible authenticity concern arises. A disclaimer such as “replica,” “mirror quality” or “inspired” does not make counterfeit goods acceptable.

### 3.3 Weapons, ammunition and weapon components
- Firearms, ammunition, explosives and explosive components.
- Unlawful or prohibited weapons and weapon accessories.
- Items designed or marketed primarily to injure, incapacitate or threaten another person, where prohibited by law or Everneed's safety rules.
- Instructions, kits or services intended to manufacture illegal weapons or explosives.

Ordinary household tools are not automatically prohibited merely because they could be misused; context, design, marketing and applicable law matter. The platform should not enable weapon categories until a specialist legal review and enforceable controls are approved.

### 3.4 Controlled drugs and unsafe intoxicating substances
- Illegal narcotics, controlled substances offered without lawful authority, and products intended for unlawful drug use.
- Illicit drug paraphernalia or products marketed to facilitate unlawful consumption or manufacture.
- Unapproved, adulterated or dangerously formulated intoxicants.
- Substances falsely presented as medicines, supplements or approved treatments.

Prescription medicines and other regulated health products are addressed under restricted categories below. This section does not authorise their sale.

### 3.5 Hazardous, toxic and dangerous materials
- Poisons, highly hazardous chemicals, restricted pesticides and unsafe chemical mixtures.
- Explosive, radioactive, biological or chemical hazard materials that Everneed is not legally and operationally authorised to handle.
- Products with credible evidence of contamination, unsafe composition or dangerous defects.
- Items recalled or formally prohibited by a competent authority, where sale or distribution is not permitted.

### 3.6 Human exploitation and abuse
- Products or services involving human trafficking, forced labour, exploitation or abuse.
- Non-consensual intimate imagery or material that exploits a person.
- Human body parts, organs, tissue or bodily materials where sale is unlawful or exploitative.
- Products or services that promote violence, abuse or exploitation.

### 3.7 Wildlife, protected species and environmental contraband
- Protected wildlife, endangered species, ivory, illegal animal parts or products derived from unlawful wildlife trade.
- Unlawfully harvested timber, protected plants, restricted natural resources or other environmental contraband.
- Live animals or animal products where lawful sourcing, welfare, transport and required approvals cannot be established.

### 3.8 Fraudulent documents, accounts and credentials
- Forged identity documents, certificates, licences, academic records or official records.
- Bank accounts, payment accounts, SIM registrations, social-media accounts or other credentials offered for unauthorised transfer or misuse.
- Stolen, compromised or unlawfully obtained digital accounts, passwords, tokens or access keys.
- Fake reviews, fabricated verification badges or paid manipulation presented as genuine customer activity.

### 3.9 Deceptive or harmful listings
- Listings that impersonate a government agency, regulator, manufacturer, brand or another seller.
- Products falsely advertised as approved, certified, clinically proven, guaranteed or endorsed.
- Listings designed to mislead buyers about the product's identity, quantity, quality, condition, origin or price.
- Pyramid, Ponzi or deceptive investment schemes presented as products or business opportunities.
- Products or services that encourage self-harm, dangerous misuse or serious harm to others.

## 4. Restricted categories — approval required before listing

The categories below are not automatically approved. Everneed must keep them disabled until it has a written category decision, legal review where needed, seller verification requirements, customer disclosures and a practical moderation process.

### 4.1 Medicines, pharmacy and medical products
Examples include prescription medicines, over-the-counter medicines, vaccines, medical devices, diagnostic products, clinical equipment and products making medical treatment claims.

Before enabling any such category, Everneed must determine:
- Which products may lawfully be sold through the proposed marketplace model.
- Whether the seller must be a licensed pharmacy, healthcare professional, importer, manufacturer or other authorised person.
- Whether product registration, listing approval, prescription validation, storage, temperature control, traceability or recall procedures apply.
- Whether the platform's role, payment flow and fulfilment arrangements are legally permissible.
- What claims and customer information may be displayed.

No seller may list prescription medicines, controlled medicines or regulated medical products until the required legal and operational controls are verified. Everneed must not imply that a product is safe or approved simply because a listing passed platform review.

### 4.2 Food, beverages and consumables
Examples include packaged foods, fresh foods, drinks, infant foods, supplements and products requiring controlled storage.

Potential requirements may include:
- Product registration or other applicable authorisation.
- Food hygiene, handling, labelling, allergen and date information.
- Batch, expiry and recall traceability.
- Temperature-controlled storage and transport where necessary.
- Seller, manufacturer or facility approvals where applicable.

Everneed must not enable products whose safety cannot be maintained through the actual storage and delivery route.

### 4.3 Cosmetics, personal care and beauty products
Examples include skincare, hair products, fragrances, cosmetics, soaps and products applied to the body.

Require appropriate checks for product identity, ingredients, labelling, safety claims, registration or notification where applicable, authenticity and responsible sourcing. Products containing prohibited substances, unsafe concentrations or misleading medical claims must be rejected.

### 4.4 Alcohol and age-restricted products
Alcoholic beverages and other age-restricted goods must remain disabled unless Everneed has verified the applicable legal requirements, licensing, age assurance, advertising restrictions, delivery controls and geographic limitations. A simple age checkbox may not be sufficient.

### 4.5 Tobacco, nicotine and related products
Tobacco, nicotine products, electronic delivery devices and related accessories require specialist legal review of product restrictions, advertising, age controls, importation, packaging and distribution. Keep these categories disabled until the exact rules and controls are confirmed. Everneed may decide to prohibit them entirely.

### 4.6 Pesticides, agricultural chemicals and veterinary products
These may require product registration, seller authorisation, hazard classification, safe packaging, storage, transport, usage instructions and restrictions on who may purchase them. Do not enable a listing where Everneed cannot verify the required controls.

### 4.7 Electrical, battery, gas and safety-critical goods
Examples include electrical appliances, chargers, power banks, batteries, generators, gas equipment, protective equipment and products used in safety-critical settings.

Require accurate specifications, safe condition, manufacturer/importer details where relevant, applicable standards or conformity evidence, warranty information and recall handling. Reject counterfeit chargers, unsafe batteries, modified electrical goods and products with credible fire, shock or injury risks.

### 4.8 Children's products and toys
Products intended for babies or children may require enhanced safety, age suitability, warnings, materials and labelling checks. Reject products with unsafe design, choking or strangulation hazards, misleading age claims or recalled status. Do not collect children's personal data beyond what is necessary and lawful.

### 4.9 Financial, investment and professional services
Loans, investments, insurance, securities, foreign-exchange services, payment services, money transfer, crypto-related services, legal services and other regulated professional services must not be listed unless the provider's authorisation, advertising, disclosures and marketplace role have been reviewed by qualified specialists.

Everneed must not facilitate unlicensed financial services or present itself as a regulated provider without the required authorisation.

### 4.10 Digital goods, software and subscriptions
Digital goods may be restricted where ownership, licensing, transferability, delivery, refund rights or account security cannot be established. Sellers must not offer stolen accounts, shared credentials, unauthorised licence keys, malware, spyware, credential-harvesting tools or circumvention services.

### 4.11 Animals, plants and agricultural products
Live animals, plants, seeds, fertilisers, animal medicines and agricultural inputs may be subject to welfare, quarantine, import/export, phytosanitary, veterinary or other controls. Require specialist review and appropriate seller evidence before enabling relevant categories.

### 4.12 Used, refurbished and second-hand goods
Used goods are not automatically prohibited. Sellers must disclose condition, defects, repairs, missing components, ownership and warranty limitations accurately. Reject goods with credible evidence of theft, unsafe modification, counterfeit status or recall restrictions.

## 5. Services, listings and promotional content

This policy also applies to service listings, bundles, free gifts, samples, advertisements, sponsored placements, store banners and promotional messages.

A service may be rejected where it is unlawful, unsafe, deceptive, unlicensed or outside the Platform's approved scope. A seller must not use a permitted product listing to advertise a prohibited product or redirect customers to complete a prohibited transaction elsewhere.

Paid placement must not disguise a restricted or prohibited listing as approved. Advertising and ranking labels must be clear and must not imply independent endorsement.

## 6. Seller declarations and evidence

A seller must:
- Confirm that it is authorised to offer the product.
- Provide accurate product identity, brand, manufacturer, source and condition information.
- Disclose ingredients, warnings, age limits, expiry dates, batch details or other category-specific information where relevant.
- Supply licences, registrations, certificates, invoices or authorisation evidence when reasonably required.
- Notify Everneed promptly if an approval expires, a product is recalled, a regulator issues a warning or the product's safety or legal status changes.
- Cooperate with reasonable authenticity, safety and compliance investigations.
- Keep records needed to trace products and respond to customer or regulator enquiries.

Everneed should request only evidence relevant to the product and risk. Documents must be submitted through a secure approved channel, not public listing text or social-media comments.

## 7. Listing review and category controls

Before a category is enabled, the designated owner should document:
1. Category definition and permitted product types.
2. Prohibited variants and known risk indicators.
3. Applicable legal and sector-regulatory requirements.
4. Seller eligibility and evidence requirements.
5. Customer-facing disclosures and warnings.
6. Storage, packaging, transport and delivery requirements.
7. Review, monitoring and recall process.
8. Refund, complaint and incident handling.
9. Staff training and escalation contacts.
10. Evidence that the product and fulfilment workflow can be supported safely.

A category may be:
- **Disabled:** sellers cannot submit or publish listings.
- **Application-only:** listings require prior seller and category approval.
- **Pre-publication review:** every listing is reviewed before publication.
- **Conditionally enabled:** only defined products and verified sellers are permitted.
- **Enabled:** ordinary listing rules apply, subject to ongoing monitoring.

The actual category state must be enforced in the production system. A category label, hidden button or policy statement is not a technical control by itself.

## 8. Moderation, reports and investigations

Everneed may review listings before publication and after publication, including in response to customer reports, seller reports, rights-holder complaints, regulator notices, recalls, safety alerts or internal risk signals.

A report should capture, where available:
- Listing and seller identifiers.
- Product name, category and relevant variant.
- Reason for the report.
- Supporting images, documents or transaction references.
- Any immediate safety concern.
- Date received and person responsible for review.

Where credible serious risk exists, Everneed may temporarily hide a listing or pause sales while it investigates. Preserve relevant records, limit access to those who need them and avoid disclosing a reporter's identity unnecessarily.

For urgent threats to life, safety or public health, escalate promptly to the designated responsible person and relevant emergency or competent authority as advised. Do not delay necessary protective action merely to complete an ordinary moderation workflow.

## 9. Enforcement and seller fairness

Possible actions include:
- Requesting more information.
- Returning a listing for correction.
- Rejecting or removing a listing.
- Disabling a product category or variant.
- Temporarily restricting new orders.
- Requiring enhanced seller verification.
- Suspending a store or account.
- Terminating seller participation for serious or repeated violations.
- Reporting suspected unlawful activity to an appropriate authority where legally required or justified.

Enforcement must be proportionate to the risk and evidence. Where safe and lawful, explain the reason, identify the relevant policy concern and give the seller an opportunity to respond or appeal.

Immediate restriction may be necessary for credible counterfeit, unlawful goods, serious safety risks, regulator action, fraud or account compromise. Everneed must not use a moderation decision as a pretext for arbitrary financial deductions or indefinite withholding of seller funds. Any financial adjustment must have a separate contractual and legal basis, be documented and be open to challenge.

An appeal should identify the listing or decision, explain why the seller believes it was incorrect and include relevant evidence. Where practical, a reviewer who did not make the original decision should assess the appeal.

Reinstatement may require corrected listing information, verified authorisation, new safety evidence, removal of misleading claims or completion of a corrective action. A product must not be restored merely because a seller disputes the decision if material legal or safety risk remains unresolved.

## 11. Recalls, safety incidents and regulator notices

Everneed should establish a documented product-safety incident process before enabling categories with meaningful safety risk.

The process should include:
- Immediate identification of affected listings, sellers, orders and product batches where possible.
- Temporary listing or sales suspension when warranted.
- Preservation of relevant evidence and transaction records.
- Prompt communication with affected sellers and customers where appropriate.
- Clear customer instructions and remedies based on the incident and applicable law.
- Cooperation with the manufacturer, importer, relevant regulator or competent authority.
- Tracking of returns, refunds, replacements, disposal or other corrective actions.
- A documented decision before a recalled or unsafe product can be relisted.

A seller must notify Everneed promptly of a recall, regulator warning, serious defect, injury report or credible contamination issue. Everneed must not claim it has a recall capability until roles, records, contacts and customer communications have been tested.

## 12. Customer transparency

Customers must receive accurate information before purchase, including where relevant:
- Product identity, seller and condition.
- Total price and any mandatory charges.
- Material specifications, size, quantity and compatibility.
- Ingredients, allergens, warnings and age restrictions.
- Expiry or use-by information where relevant.
- Warranty, return and refund terms.
- Delivery limitations and realistic fulfilment expectations.
- Whether a product is refurbished, used, imported or subject to a material limitation.

A seller must not conceal material facts in images, footnotes or post-payment messages. Everneed should remove misleading claims and correct customer-facing information promptly.

## 13. Data handling and confidentiality

Product compliance records may contain seller identity, business, sourcing, licence and incident information. Collect and retain only what is reasonably needed for the relevant compliance purpose.

Access must be role-based. Do not publish seller identity documents, private supplier invoices, customer reports or investigation records. Handle personal data in accordance with the Privacy Notice, applicable data-protection law and any relevant contractual obligations.

## 15. Internal readiness checklist

### Legal and category review
- [ ] Obtain Nigerian legal review of prohibited and restricted categories.
- [ ] Map category requirements to current federal and state rules, product type and transaction route.
- [ ] Identify competent regulators and confirm current requirements directly from official sources.
- [ ] Decide which restricted categories Everneed will prohibit entirely.
- [ ] Confirm any required licences, registrations, import authorisations, standards or age controls.

### Product and seller controls
- [ ] Build a category taxonomy with disabled-by-default restricted categories.
- [ ] Create seller declarations and category-specific evidence checklists.
- [ ] Define authenticity, counterfeit, recall and product-safety review procedures.
- [ ] Ensure sellers can correct ordinary listing errors and appeal decisions.
- [ ] Train reviewers to recognise risk indicators without making unsupported legal determinations.

### Technical controls
- [ ] Enforce category restrictions server-side, not only in the user interface.
- [ ] Test that prohibited categories cannot be created through API or direct database calls.
- [ ] Test listing review, removal, suspension, appeal and audit logging.
- [ ] Prevent restricted products from appearing in search, recommendations, promotions and feeds.
- [ ] Provide a customer and rights-holder reporting route.
- [ ] Restrict access to verification and investigation records.

### Operations and customer protection
- [ ] Define product recall and urgent safety escalation contacts.
- [ ] Confirm storage and delivery capability before enabling perishable, hazardous or temperature-sensitive products.
- [ ] Align complaints, returns and refunds with the relevant category risks.
- [ ] Confirm customer disclosures and total-price presentation.
- [ ] Test incident handling from report through resolution and customer communication.

### Controlled rollout
- [ ] Start with lower-risk categories that can be reviewed and supported.
- [ ] Keep restricted categories disabled until each gate is evidenced.
- [ ] Review incident trends, rejected listings, customer complaints and seller appeals.
- [ ] Reassess the policy whenever the product, geography, fulfilment model or law changes.
