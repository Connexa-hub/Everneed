## 1. Purpose

This document records Everneed's founding-stage leadership position, clarifies accountability, and establishes how the founding team should develop as the business moves from product development into validation, pilot operations and growth.

It distinguishes the people and responsibilities that are confirmed from roles that are only proposed. It is not a public staff directory, legal ownership register or claim that Everneed already has a staffed executive team.

## 2. Founding-Stage Position

Everneed is currently a founder-led startup project.

The founder is the person setting the company's direction, making final decisions and coordinating the development of the business and platform. The company profile and organisational plan describe a future team structure, but do not establish that additional co-founders, directors, executives or employees have been appointed.

**Current confirmed leadership model:**
- **Founder:** overall company leadership, strategy, priorities and final decisions.

No additional co-founder or executive appointment is recorded in this document. Names, titles, equity interests and legal appointments must only be added after the relevant people and arrangements have been expressly confirmed.

## 3. Founder Responsibilities

The founder holds overall accountability for:

### Company direction
- Define Everneed's vision, mission, positioning and strategic priorities.
- Decide which markets, customer segments and business opportunities to pursue.
- Keep the company focused on proving the initial marketplace before expanding into additional services.
- Approve material changes to the business model and company direction.

### Product and technology oversight
- Set product priorities and acceptance expectations.
- Coordinate product development and review progress.
- Ensure that product claims match implemented and verified capabilities.
- Approve major releases and significant changes to the customer, seller and administrator experience.
- Ensure security, reliability and user experience are treated as core requirements.

### Commercial and financial oversight
- Review operating costs, budgets and financial commitments.
- Approve commercial principles, seller terms and pricing after validation.
- Monitor marketplace and fulfilment unit economics.
- Protect company resources and maintain clear records of financial decisions.

### Market and relationship development
- Lead early seller and customer discovery.
- Build relationships with prospective partners, advisers and service providers.
- Represent Everneed accurately to potential collaborators and investors.
- Gather market feedback and convert it into practical priorities.

### People and governance
- Define responsibilities and appoint accountable owners.
- Approve recruitment and contractor engagements.
- Establish decision-making, reporting and access-control practices.
- Seek qualified legal, accounting, tax and regulatory advice where required.

The founder may delegate execution, but delegation should include a clear scope, expected outcome, authority limit and reporting responsibility.

## 4. Product Development and External Contributors

Everneed's development work may involve external developers, contractors, technical advisers and AI-assisted development tools.

These contributors must be described accurately:

- A contractor is not automatically a co-founder, director or employee.
- An adviser is not automatically an executive or company representative.
- An AI development tool or assistant is not a human team member, legal person, co-founder or owner.
- Use of a tool does not transfer company ownership or accountability away from the founder.

The current development workflow uses Claude as a primary AI-assisted coding contributor. This is a development-tool arrangement, not a human founding-team appointment.

For every external contributor, Everneed should record:
- Name or provider and engagement type.
- Scope of work and deliverables.
- Start date and, where relevant, end date.
- Payment or compensation terms.
- Confidentiality and intellectual-property arrangements.
- Repository, infrastructure and data access granted.
- Review and acceptance responsibility.
- Access revocation and handover requirements when the engagement ends.

All production access, secrets, financial permissions and sensitive customer or seller data should be granted only when necessary and under controlled access arrangements.

## 5. Founding Leadership Principles

The founding team should be built around the following principles:

1. **Mission before titles:** recruit people who can solve real company problems, not simply fill an organisational chart.
2. **Clear accountability:** each critical outcome has one accountable owner.
3. **Founder-led strategic control:** major changes to company direction, ownership, financing and material commitments remain subject to founder approval and any applicable legal governance.
4. **Execution with evidence:** progress is measured by working outcomes, customer value and operational results.
5. **Integrity in representation:** never imply that a role, partnership, registration, product capability or milestone exists before it is confirmed.
6. **Security by responsibility:** access to code, production systems, money and private data is limited according to role.
7. **Lean team development:** use contractors and specialist advisers where appropriate before creating permanent positions that the business cannot sustainably support.
8. **Respectful and inclusive work:** establish fair expectations, professional conduct, transparent communication and a safe working environment.

## 6. Future Leadership Roles

The following are proposed functions that may become leadership positions as Everneed grows. They are not current appointments.

| Proposed role | Primary accountability | When the role becomes necessary |
|---|---|---|
| Technical Lead / Head of Engineering | Architecture, engineering quality, security, releases and technical delivery | When development complexity and contributor count require dedicated technical leadership |
| Product Lead | Product strategy, discovery, roadmap, requirements and acceptance | When product priorities and cross-team coordination exceed founder capacity |
| Head of Marketplace Operations | Seller onboarding, catalogue quality, customer support and marketplace standards | When active sellers and customer cases require consistent operational ownership |
| Logistics & Fulfilment Lead | Hub procedures, package custody, delivery partners, exceptions and logistics economics | Before a physical hub or regular fulfilment operation begins |
| Growth & Partnerships Lead | Seller acquisition, customer growth, brand distribution and partnerships | When repeatable acquisition channels and partnership workload justify ownership |
| Finance & Administration Lead | Accounting, cash controls, reconciliations, reporting and administration | When transaction volume, payroll or financial complexity requires dedicated oversight |

A person may initially cover more than one function. This should not create confusion about approval limits or control of sensitive processes.

## 7. Initial Team-Building Sequence

Recruitment should follow the company's actual stage and bottlenecks.

### Stage 1 — Founder-led development
Priorities:
- Complete and validate the marketplace foundation.
- Use clearly scoped development support.
- Obtain specialist legal, accounting and security advice when required.
- Conduct direct seller and customer discovery.
- Avoid unnecessary fixed payroll.

### Stage 2 — Controlled pilot preparation
Potential first additions:
- Marketplace operations / seller success support.
- Quality assurance and device testing.
- Customer support coverage.
- Technical support for release, security and reliability.
- Logistics operations support only when a real pilot model and location are selected.

### Stage 3 — Repeatable marketplace operations
Potential additions:
- Dedicated engineering and product leadership.
- Seller acquisition and growth.
- Marketplace moderation and support.
- Finance and reconciliation.
- Hub supervisor and fulfilment associates if a physical hub is operating regularly.

### Stage 4 — Multi-market expansion
Potential additions:
- Regional operations leadership.
- Partnerships and country launch management.
- Logistics network management.
- Compliance and finance specialists.
- Dedicated data, reliability and security capabilities.

Hiring decisions must consider validated workload, runway, affordability, expected impact and the cost of not filling the role.

## 8. Decision Rights and Accountability

Until formal delegation is documented, the founder retains final internal approval for:

- Company strategy and market entry.
- Product scope and release priorities.
- Material expenditure and financial commitments.
- Seller commercial terms and pricing policy.
- Key partnerships and external representation.
- Hiring, contractor engagement and compensation commitments.
- Production access and security-sensitive permissions.
- Fundraising discussions and financing commitments.
- Legal registration and ownership decisions.

Future leaders should receive written authority appropriate to their role. No individual should independently approve a transaction, refund, payout, contract or production change outside their assigned authority.

For sensitive workflows, Everneed should separate initiation, review and approval wherever team size permits. Where a small team makes separation impractical, compensating founder review and documented audit trails should be used.

## 9. Equity, Ownership and Founder Agreements

This document does not establish share ownership, equity percentages, vesting, director appointments or intellectual-property assignments.

Before adding a co-founder or issuing equity, Everneed should obtain appropriate legal and tax advice and document:

- The person's actual contribution and responsibilities.
- Equity allocation and vesting, if applicable.
- Roles, decision rights and reserved matters.
- Intellectual-property ownership and assignment.
- Confidentiality and non-disclosure obligations.
- Departure, incapacity and dispute procedures.
- Treatment of unvested equity and company assets.
- Applicable registration, filings and shareholder documentation.

A job title, informal promise or contribution to development should not be treated as an agreed ownership interest without proper documentation.

## 10. Professional Advisers and Service Providers

Everneed may need support from professionals without appointing them as employees or executives.

Potential advisory and service areas include:
- Corporate and commercial legal advice.
- Nigerian tax and accounting.
- Data protection and privacy.
- Cybersecurity and independent technical review.
- Payments and financial reconciliation.
- Logistics and fulfilment operations.
- Branding, marketing and market research.

Each engagement should have a defined scope, confidentiality expectations, deliverables, fees and limits on authority. Advisers should not be presented publicly as directors, employees or official representatives unless that status is accurate and authorised.

## 11. Team Performance and Reporting

As the team grows, each function should report on outcomes rather than activity alone.

| Function | Example measures |
|---|---|
| Product & Technology | Release quality, critical defects, uptime, workflow completion and security findings |
| Marketplace Operations | Approved active sellers, listing quality, seller response and customer issue resolution |
| Logistics & Fulfilment | Package accuracy, handover time, delivery success, exceptions and cost per consignment |
| Growth & Partnerships | Qualified sellers, active customers, acquisition cost, repeat usage and partner performance |
| Finance & Administration | Cash position, reconciliations, outstanding liabilities, payout accuracy and budget variance |

Targets should be set only after a baseline is measured. Proposed targets must be labelled as targets, not reported as achieved results.

## 12. Immediate Founding-Team Priorities

1. Maintain a clear record of the founder's responsibilities and current decision authority.
2. Identify the most important capability gaps for the next development and validation stage.
3. Keep AI-assisted development, contractors, advisers and prospective employees clearly distinguished.
4. Prepare written scopes and intellectual-property terms for external contributors.
5. Avoid appointing executive titles before responsibilities and authority are agreed.
6. Identify specialist legal and accounting support for the company's formal establishment.
7. Recruit only when a role addresses a validated bottleneck and can be sustainably funded.
