# EVERNEED
## Recruitment & Talent Acquisition Plan

**Document type:** Internal recruitment and workforce planning framework  
**Document status:** Proposed hiring plan; no unconfirmed vacancies or appointments implied  
**Business status:** Founder-led startup; not yet registered with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)  
**Initial market:** Nigeria  
**Project reference branch:** `web-portal`  
**Last updated:** November 2026

## 1. Purpose

This plan explains how Everneed should identify, assess, engage and onboard people as the business moves from founder-led development into marketplace validation, controlled operations and growth.

Recruitment must solve real capacity or capability gaps. It should not create payroll commitments simply to make the organisation look larger.

This is an internal working plan. It is not a public vacancy announcement, evidence that funding is available, or confirmation that any role is currently open.

## 2. Recruitment Principles

1. **Hire against a verified need:** identify the work, bottleneck and expected outcome before creating a role.
2. **Protect runway:** prefer affordable, time-bounded support where the workload is temporary or uncertain.
3. **Prioritise customer and seller outcomes:** hire people who improve marketplace reliability, trust, fulfilment or sustainable growth.
4. **Use clear role ownership:** every engagement has a scope, supervisor, deliverables and review process.
5. **Assess practical ability:** use relevant work samples and structured interviews rather than relying only on certificates or claims.
6. **Recruit fairly:** communicate requirements and selection criteria clearly, treat candidates respectfully and avoid discriminatory screening.
7. **Protect the business:** use appropriate contracts, confidentiality, intellectual-property assignment, access controls and handover procedures.
8. **Do not overstate status:** distinguish a proposed role, published vacancy, selected candidate, signed engagement and active team member.

## 3. Hiring Readiness Gate

Before approving any hire or contractor, the founder should document:

- The business problem or workload requiring additional capacity.
- Why the work cannot reasonably be handled through reprioritisation, automation, a service provider or a short specialist engagement.
- The outcomes and measurable deliverables expected.
- Required skills and the minimum experience needed.
- Whether the role is employee, fixed-term, part-time, freelance, consultant, agency or volunteer.
- The proposed compensation and full cost of engagement.
- Available budget and the effect on cash runway.
- Who will supervise the person and review their work.
- What company, customer, seller, financial or production information they need to access.
- Contract, tax, labour, data protection and intellectual-property requirements that need professional review.

Do not make an offer until the scope, budget, engagement type and approval authority are clear. Obtain qualified Nigerian legal and accounting advice where classification, payroll, tax or statutory obligations are uncertain.

## 4. Hiring Priorities by Stage

The sequence below is a planning guide, not a commitment to hire on fixed dates.

### Stage A — Founder-led development and validation

**Primary objective:** complete and verify the core marketplace before building a large team.

Potential needs:
- Scoped software engineering support where a specific technical task exceeds available capacity.
- Independent QA and Android device testing before important releases.
- Occasional UI/UX design support for defined usability problems.
- Specialist security, privacy, legal and accounting advice.
- Founder-led seller and customer discovery.

Avoid permanent roles whose workload is not yet demonstrated. AI-assisted development tools may support work, but they do not replace accountable human review, testing or operational ownership.

### Stage B — Controlled pilot preparation

**Priority capabilities:**
1. Marketplace operations and seller onboarding.
2. QA / release testing.
3. Customer support preparation.
4. Technical maintenance and security support.
5. Logistics process design and pilot coordination, but only after a real service area, hub approach and delivery arrangements are selected.

Combine responsibilities only where workload and risk allow. A person who handles customer support should not automatically receive unrestricted access to financial or administrative controls.

### Stage C — Early operating marketplace

Consider dedicated capacity when actual activity supports it:
- Seller success and catalogue operations.
- Customer support and case resolution.
- Fulfilment / hub supervision and package handling, if a physical hub is operating.
- Delivery coordination and partner management.
- Finance operations and reconciliation.
- Engineering and product ownership.

### Stage D — Repeatable growth and expansion

Add specialised leadership or teams only when sustained volume, geographic coverage and financial capacity justify them:
- Engineering leadership and dedicated product management.
- Growth, partnerships and seller acquisition.
- Regional marketplace operations.
- Logistics network management.
- Finance, compliance, people operations and data capabilities.

Expansion hiring should follow proven service quality in the existing market, not precede it.

## 5. Role Priority Matrix

| Priority | Role / capability | Main purpose | Hiring trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | QA / device-testing support | Test critical customer, seller and admin workflows across supported devices | Before inviting a meaningful pilot cohort or releasing high-risk changes |
| P0 | Marketplace operations / seller success | Onboard sellers, improve catalogue readiness and coordinate seller procedures | When founder-led onboarding and follow-up become a measurable bottleneck |
| P0 | Customer support | Respond to customer questions and coordinate issue resolution | Before live transactions create support obligations beyond founder capacity |
| P1 | Scoped software engineer | Deliver defined technical work and resolve engineering bottlenecks | When a prioritised technical task has clear scope, acceptance criteria and budget |
| P1 | Logistics / fulfilment coordinator | Manage package handovers, hub workflow and delivery exceptions | Before a real hub-assisted pilot or regular physical fulfilment begins |
| P1 | Finance / reconciliation support | Maintain accurate records and reconcile collections, refunds, costs and settlements | Before transaction volume or payout activity makes manual founder-only controls unsafe |
| P2 | Growth / partnerships associate | Run measured seller and customer acquisition activities | When the marketplace has reliable supply and fulfilment capacity to serve additional demand |
| P2 | Product manager | Coordinate discovery, requirements, prioritisation and acceptance | When cross-functional product workload consistently exceeds founder capacity |
| P2 | Technical lead | Own engineering standards, architecture, security and delivery | When engineering complexity and contributor count justify dedicated leadership |
| P3 | Regional operations / country lead | Establish and manage a new operating area | Only after expansion approval and local operating readiness |

P0 indicates a capability needed for the next relevant readiness gate; it does not mean every position must be a full-time employee immediately.

## 6. Role Scorecards

Use a written scorecard for each approved role. The following are starting templates to adapt to the actual vacancy.

### 6.1 Marketplace Operations / Seller Success

**Mission:** help suitable sellers become operational and maintain accurate, reliable storefronts.

**Responsibilities**
- Respond to seller enquiries and explain onboarding steps.
- Track applications, approvals, store setup and catalogue readiness.
- Help sellers provide accurate product information, images, prices and stock status.
- Explain order preparation, package identification and handover expectations.
- Follow up on inactive or blocked sellers.
- Record feedback and recurring seller problems.
- Escalate suspected fraud, unsafe products or policy breaches.

**Desired capabilities**
- Clear written and spoken communication.
- Strong organisation and follow-through.
- Customer service or retail experience.
- Comfort using mobile apps, spreadsheets and messaging tools.
- Good judgement when handling seller information.

**Evidence of success**
- Shorter onboarding delays.
- More approved sellers reaching catalogue-ready status.
- Accurate and current product listings.
- Fewer avoidable seller handover errors.
- Seller issues recorded and resolved within agreed service targets.

### 6.2 QA / Device Testing Support

**Mission:** find defects before customers, sellers or administrators encounter them.

**Responsibilities**
- Execute documented test cases for registration, login, discovery, store setup, product submission, cart, checkout and order journeys.
- Test supported Android screen sizes, browsers and WebViews.
- Test slow, interrupted and restored network conditions.
- Record reproducible defects with device, browser, steps, expected result, actual result and evidence.
- Retest fixes and run regression checks.
- Escalate security, payment and data-access concerns immediately.

**Desired capabilities**
- Careful observation and structured reporting.
- Practical Android and browser testing.
- Ability to follow test cases consistently.
- Basic understanding of web/mobile application behaviour.
- Clear communication with developers.

**Evidence of success**
- Critical user journeys have repeatable test evidence.
- Defects are reported clearly and retested.
- Release-blocking issues are identified before deployment.

### 6.3 Customer Support

**Mission:** provide clear, respectful assistance and ensure customer problems reach the correct owner.

**Responsibilities**
- Answer product, account, order and delivery enquiries.
- Record and classify support cases.
- Coordinate with sellers and fulfilment operators on delayed, missing, incorrect or damaged packages.
- Explain approved cancellation, return and refund procedures.
- Escalate payment disputes, safety concerns and suspected account compromise.
- Protect personal information and avoid unsupported promises.

**Desired capabilities**
- Patient and professional communication.
- Accurate recordkeeping.
- Calm problem-solving.
- Ability to follow escalation procedures.
- Responsible handling of personal and transaction information.

**Evidence of success**
- Cases acknowledged and resolved within approved service targets.
- Accurate case records and handovers.
- Reduced repeat complaints caused by unclear communication.

### 6.4 Logistics / Fulfilment Coordinator

**Mission:** maintain package traceability and coordinate reliable movement through the approved fulfilment process.

**Responsibilities**
- Coordinate seller package drop-offs or collections.
- Record package receipt, seller reference, order reference, count and visible condition.
- Track custody at each handover.
- Coordinate checking, sorting, consolidation, packaging and dispatch where applicable.
- Maintain exception records for missing, damaged, delayed or mismatched packages.
- Coordinate delivery partners and proof of delivery.
- Support returns and failed-delivery procedures.

**Desired capabilities**
- Strong attention to detail.
- Logistics, warehouse, dispatch or retail operations experience.
- Reliable communication and recordkeeping.
- Ability to follow custody and safety procedures.
- Practical knowledge of the selected service area, when relevant.

**Evidence of success**
- Accurate package reconciliation.
- Fewer lost or mismatched packages.
- Measured hub turnaround and delivery performance.
- Complete handover and exception records.

This role should not be recruited as an operational hub position until the hub model, location, responsibilities and pilot budget are confirmed.

### 6.5 Software Engineer / Technical Contributor

**Mission:** deliver secure, maintainable and testable software against an agreed scope.

**Responsibilities**
- Implement assigned features or fixes.
- Follow repository and branching practices.
- Write or update relevant tests and documentation.
- Review security, permissions, validation and error handling.
- Provide clear progress updates and handover notes.
- Avoid exposing secrets or copying production data into uncontrolled environments.

**Desired capabilities**
- Demonstrable experience with the required stack.
- Secure coding and debugging skills.
- Ability to explain trade-offs.
- Good version-control practices.
- Reliable communication and documentation.

**Evidence of success**
- Accepted deliverables meet written acceptance criteria.
- Changes pass relevant automated and manual tests.
- No unauthorised access or secret exposure.
- Work can be maintained by another qualified contributor.

A contributor must not receive production credentials or broad database access simply because they can write code.

## 7. Engagement Types

Select the engagement type that fits the work and applicable law.

| Type | Suitable use | Controls |
|---|---|---|
| Employee | Ongoing work under continuing company direction and an established role | Written employment terms, clear duties, compensation and applicable statutory compliance |
| Fixed-term employee | Defined period of continuing work | Written term, end date, renewal decision and applicable employment obligations |
| Independent contractor / freelancer | Specific deliverables with defined scope and acceptance criteria | Services agreement, milestones, payment terms, IP assignment and confidentiality |
| Consultant / adviser | Specialist advice or review | Defined advisory scope, conflicts disclosure, deliverables and limits on authority |
| Agency / service provider | Outsourced specialist or operational service | Service levels, data handling, subcontracting, liability and exit provisions |
| Internship / trainee | Structured learning and supervised practical work | Clear learning objectives, supervision, fair treatment and legal compliance |

Do not label someone a contractor merely to avoid employment obligations. Actual working arrangements and applicable law matter. Avoid unpaid work that substitutes for an ordinary productive role without a lawful and fair arrangement.

## 8. Recruitment Channels and Candidate Sourcing

Potential channels include:
- Founder and professional referrals.
- Nigerian technology, business and university communities.
- Relevant professional associations and local networks.
- Reputable job boards and professional platforms.
- Portfolio communities and open-source contributions for technical roles.
- Specialist agencies for roles where screening expertise is valuable.

Choose channels based on the role and candidate quality, not reach alone. Verify any recruiter or agency before paying fees or sharing candidate information.

For early roles, a clear role brief shared through relevant networks may be more effective and less costly than a broad public campaign.

## 9. Selection Process

Use a consistent process for each vacancy.

1. **Role approval:** confirm need, scope, budget, engagement type and reporting owner.
2. **Role brief:** publish responsibilities, essential skills, work arrangement, location expectations, compensation information where approved, and selection stages.
3. **Application review:** assess only role-relevant experience and evidence.
4. **Initial conversation:** discuss motivation, availability, communication and expectations.
5. **Practical assessment:** use a short, realistic task related to the role. Explain the time required and do not use unpaid assessments as a substitute for commercial work.
6. **Structured interview:** ask candidates the same core competency questions and score against the role criteria.
7. **Reference and work verification:** with appropriate consent, verify relevant references, portfolio claims and credentials proportionately.
8. **Decision:** document the reasons for selection and any material risks.
9. **Written offer or agreement:** confirm duties, compensation, start date, confidentiality, IP, data handling, termination and other relevant terms.
10. **Onboarding:** provide training, access and first deliverables.

Candidates should receive respectful updates. Retain applicant data only as long as there is a legitimate purpose and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

## 10. Assessment and Scoring Framework

Use a role-specific scorecard. A suggested general weighting is:

| Assessment area | Suggested weight |
|---|---:|
| Relevant practical skills and work evidence | 30% |
| Problem-solving and judgement | 20% |
| Communication and collaboration | 15% |
| Reliability, ownership and follow-through | 15% |
| Role-specific knowledge and learning ability | 10% |
| Security, confidentiality and professional conduct | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

Score each area against evidence, not personal impressions. Adjust weights for specialist roles. A serious integrity, safety or security concern may disqualify a candidate regardless of the numerical score.

## 11. Compensation and Budget Controls

No salary, contractor rate, equity grant or benefits package is approved by this document.

Before advertising compensation or making an offer:
- Research current rates for the role, experience level and location.
- Compare employee total cost with contractor or agency cost.
- Include equipment, connectivity, transport, training, statutory obligations and management time where relevant.
- Define payment frequency, deliverable milestones and expense approval.
- Confirm affordability under a conservative cash-flow scenario.
- Avoid promising equity, future salary increases or bonuses without written approval and proper documentation.

The founder should approve every recurring payroll commitment and material contractor engagement. Do not fund hiring from customer payment collections or seller funds that are owed to another party.

## 12. Candidate Onboarding

Every new team member or contractor should receive an onboarding plan appropriate to their access and responsibilities.

### Before starting
- Signed agreement and required documentation.
- Confirmed manager and communication channels.
- Role objectives, deliverables and review dates.
- Confidentiality and intellectual-property terms.
- Data protection and security expectations.
- Approved equipment and access list.

### First week
- Explain Everneed's mission, marketplace model and current product status.
- Review the role's scope, decision rights and escalation routes.
- Provide only the access needed for assigned work.
- Explain repository, support, seller or fulfilment procedures relevant to the role.
- Agree the first deliverable and how it will be accepted.

### First 30 days or initial engagement period
- Review work quality, reliability and communication.
- Confirm that access is appropriate.
- Identify training or process gaps.
- Document feedback and agreed improvements.
- Decide whether to continue, adjust scope or end the engagement in accordance with the agreement.

The review period should be stated in the relevant agreement; this plan does not impose a universal probation period.

## 13. Confidentiality, Intellectual Property and Access

Before work begins, use appropriate written terms covering:
- Confidential business information.
- Customer and seller personal data.
- Source code, designs, documentation and other work product.
- Ownership or assignment of intellectual property, as legally appropriate.
- Use of third-party and open-source materials.
- Restrictions on sharing credentials or production information.
- Incident reporting and return or deletion of company information at the end of engagement.

Apply least-privilege access:
- Use individual accounts; do not share passwords.
- Enable multi-factor authentication where available.
- Keep production, financial and administrative permissions restricted.
- Use test data for development and QA wherever possible.
- Do not expose API keys, payment secrets or customer records in public repositories or messaging groups.
- Review access periodically and revoke it promptly when no longer required.

For source-code contributions, document repository permissions, review requirements, accepted changes and handover expectations.

## 14. Performance Management

Set expectations before work begins. Review outcomes using role-specific measures.

| Function | Example measures |
|---|---|
| Seller success | Seller onboarding completion, catalogue readiness, seller response and retention |
| QA | Critical workflow coverage, reproducible defect quality, regression completion |
| Customer support | Response and resolution time, case quality, repeat-contact rate |
| Fulfilment | Package accuracy, custody-record completeness, hub turnaround, delivery exceptions |
| Engineering | Accepted deliverables, defect rate, test coverage relevant to changes, documentation and reliability |
| Growth | Qualified seller leads, activated sellers, customer conversion, retention and acquisition cost |
| Finance | Reconciliation timeliness, record accuracy, unresolved variances and control exceptions |

Targets should be set only after baseline measurement. Do not reward activity counts that encourage low-quality seller registrations, misleading customer acquisition or unsafe delivery shortcuts.

## 15. Nigeria-Specific Recruitment and Compliance Considerations

Everneed's initial market is Nigeria. Before employing people or engaging contractors, obtain current professional guidance on applicable obligations, which may include:
- Employment terms and applicable labour requirements.
- PAYE and other tax treatment, as applicable.
- Pension, insurance, workplace safety and other statutory requirements where applicable.
- Data privacy obligations when processing candidate, employee, seller or customer information.
- Proper business registration and contracting capacity before entering formal commercial arrangements.
- State-specific or sector-specific requirements relevant to the actual operating location and activity.

Requirements can depend on the engagement structure, workforce size, location and current law. This document is not legal, tax or employment advice. Verify current requirements with qualified Nigerian professionals and relevant authorities before acting.

## 16. Recruitment Risks and Controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Hiring before product-market or operational need is clear | Use the hiring readiness gate and stage-based triggers |
| Payroll exceeds available runway | Model total cost and recurring commitments before approval |
| Candidate exaggerates experience or portfolio | Use practical assessments and proportionate verification |
| Unclear contractor / employee classification | Assess the real working relationship and obtain legal advice |
| Source code or customer data is exposed | Least-privilege access, written terms, secure tooling and access reviews |
| Founder becomes a bottleneck | Delegate defined outcomes with explicit approval limits |
| Role overlaps create conflict | Assign one accountable owner for each critical workflow |
| Hub staff are recruited before hub operations exist | Confirm location, process, volume and budget first |
| Growth hires generate demand the platform cannot serve | Require product, seller and fulfilment readiness gates |
| Candidate information is retained or shared improperly | Limit collection, access and retention; follow privacy requirements |

## 17. Recruitment Approval and Records

Maintain an internal recruitment register with:
- Role title and department.
- Business need and hiring trigger.
- Approval date and approving person.
- Engagement type and budget.
- Role brief and scorecard.
- Recruitment channel.
- Candidate stage and decision record.
- Offer/agreement status.
- Start date and manager, if engaged.
- Access granted and later revoked.
- Review date and outcome.

Restrict access to candidate records. Do not place identity documents, private contact details, reference notes or compensation information in public repositories.

## 19. Review and Change Control

Review this plan when:
- The product moves into a controlled pilot.
- Seller, customer or fulfilment workload changes materially.
- Funding or operating runway changes.
- A new market or physical operating location is approved.
- Employment, tax, privacy or other relevant legal requirements change.

Record material changes and the evidence supporting them. Update role priorities when the actual business bottleneck changes.
