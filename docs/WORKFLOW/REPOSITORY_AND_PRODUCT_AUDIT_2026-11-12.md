## 1. What Everneed is

Everneed is a founder-led digital commerce platform, beginning with a multivendor marketplace for Nigeria. It is not a single online store selling its own catalogue. Independent sellers operate their own storefronts and list their own products; shoppers discover products across those sellers in the Everneed customer app. Everneed's seller and admin web portal supports onboarding, catalogue review and platform operations.

The product has connected surfaces:

- **Customer Android app:** shopping, product detail, cart, checkout, account and order journey.
- **Public website:** explains Everneed and directs prospective sellers and partners.
- **Seller portal:** store application, store profile and product submission/management.
- **Admin console:** seller and product review, store management and platform oversight.
- **Shared backend:** Supabase Auth, PostgreSQL, RLS, Storage, RPCs and Edge Functions.
- **Payments:** Paystack integration path; live payment and settlement readiness still require end-to-end evidence.

The public website is not intended to duplicate the customer app's catalogue, cart or checkout. The current product tracker explicitly keeps customer shopping app-first.

## 3. Theme and design contract

The shared design system is authoritative:
- Six palettes: Emerald, Indigo, Rose, Violet, Ocean and Gold.
- Three modes: Light, Dark and AMOLED.
- Emerald is the default.
- Bricolage Grotesque is the display face; Figtree is the body/UI face.
- Tokens are generated by tools/build-tokens.mjs into css/tokens.css. Do not hand-edit generated tokens.
- Website and app share theme behaviour through js/theme.js and shared build packaging.
- UI should use CSS variables such as --bg, --surface-1, --text-1, --brand, --brand-ink, --success, --info, --accent and --danger. Avoid fixed colours that break palette or dark-mode support.
- Respect mobile-first constraints: 360–412 px screens, older Android WebViews, limited mobile data, strong contrast and 44 px touch targets.
- Motion should be restrained and purposeful; honour prefers-reduced-motion.

## 5. Authentication architecture and owner setup

- Supabase Auth owns user identity, passwords, verification, OAuth and sessions.
- The website currently implements email/password signup, six-digit signup verification, resend, password login, session handling and profile-based role routing.
- The confirmation email template must include Supabase's {{ .Token }} variable because the screen expects a six-digit code.
- Resend is a transactional SMTP delivery option; it does not replace Supabase Auth.
- OAuth provider buttons read the public Supabase Auth settings endpoint and only show as available when the provider is enabled. Disabled options remain grey and explain that they are unavailable.
- Google, Apple and Facebook credentials have not been confirmed as configured. A button is not proof of a working provider.
- Owner setup steps are documented separately in docs/OWNER_GUIDES/ACCOUNT_SIGN_IN_EMAIL_AND_OAUTH.md.
- The older technical checklist remains in docs/WORKFLOW/AUTH_PRODUCTION_SETUP.md.
- Staging must be configured and tested first; production SMTP/OAuth settings are separate.
