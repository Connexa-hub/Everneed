# EVERNEED
## A Digital Commerce Ecosystem

**Company Foundation Profile**  
**Document status:** Foundational working document  
**Business status:** Founder-led startup; not yet registered with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)  
**Initial market:** Nigeria  
**Long-term ambition:** Africa and international markets  
**Last updated:** November 2026

---

## 1. Company Overview

Everneed is a technology startup building a digital commerce ecosystem designed to connect shoppers, independent sellers and businesses through accessible digital infrastructure.

The company aims to make it easier for businesses to establish their online presence, reach customers, manage their stores and participate in the growing digital economy.

Everneed's initial product is a multivendor digital marketplace that allows independent sellers to operate their own stores within one connected platform. The long-term ambition is to become a broader commerce ecosystem rather than only an online shopping platform.

## 2. Vision

To build a connected digital commerce ecosystem that empowers businesses, simplifies commerce and makes economic opportunities more accessible across Africa and beyond.

## 3. Mission

To provide accessible digital infrastructure, marketplace opportunities and business tools that help independent sellers establish, operate and grow their businesses while making shopping more convenient for customers.

## 4. The Problem

Many small and independent businesses face challenges when attempting to establish and grow their digital presence. These include:

- Limited access to affordable digital storefronts.
- Difficulty reaching customers beyond their immediate locations.
- Fragmented tools for managing online businesses.
- Challenges establishing customer trust.
- Complicated online selling processes.
- Limited access to digital commerce infrastructure.

Customers also face challenges discovering reliable sellers, comparing products and purchasing from different businesses conveniently.

## 5. Our Solution

Everneed aims to address these challenges through a connected digital commerce platform. Its initial marketplace is intended to provide:

- A shared marketplace for multiple independent sellers.
- Individual digital storefronts for businesses.
- Product discovery and shopping.
- Seller identity and store visibility.
- Customer accounts and order management.
- Connected checkout and payment experiences.
- Seller management tools.
- Administrative oversight and platform management.

The objective is to create an environment where businesses can operate independently while benefiting from shared commerce infrastructure.

## 6. Products and Services

### 6.1 Everneed Marketplace

The first major product: a multivendor marketplace connecting shoppers with independent businesses and their products.

### 6.2 Everneed Web Portal

The web-based business interface supporting the platform. Its intended functions include public company and marketplace information, seller registration and onboarding, seller storefront management, product submission and management, administrative management, and business information and support.

### 6.3 Future Ecosystem Services

These are long-term opportunities, not claims of existing products:

- Seller business tools.
- Promoted listings and advertising.
- Business subscriptions.
- Delivery and logistics partnerships.
- Commerce integrations.
- Additional digital services for businesses.

Expansion will depend on marketplace adoption, customer demand and operational capacity.

## 7. Target Market

### Initial Market: Nigeria

Everneed will initially focus on Nigerian shoppers, independent retailers, small businesses and entrepreneurs.

### Potential Future Markets

- Ghana.
- Kenya.
- South Africa.
- United Kingdom.
- United States.

International expansion will be approached progressively, taking into account local regulations, payment infrastructure, logistics and market demand.

## 8. Business Model

Everneed intends to generate revenue through a combination of commerce-related services.

Potential revenue streams include:

1. **Seller subscriptions:** Paid access to enhanced storefronts and seller services.
2. **Transaction commissions:** A platform commission on eligible completed transactions.
3. **Promoted listings:** Paid product visibility and promotion.
4. **Advertising:** Commercial advertising opportunities within the ecosystem.
5. **Business services:** Additional tools and services for merchants.

These revenue streams remain subject to validation, pricing research and financial modelling. The initial priority is to establish a useful marketplace, attract sellers and customers, and validate sustainable transaction activity.

## 9. Competitive Positioning

Everneed aims to differentiate itself through seller-focused, accessible and expandable commerce infrastructure.

Its strategic principles are:

- Make digital selling accessible to smaller businesses.
- Give sellers recognizable storefront identities.
- Make products and sellers discoverable.
- Make shopping convenient for customers.
- Prioritise mobile accessibility.
- Expand services based on real business needs.

Everneed will not attempt to introduce every ecosystem service at once. The marketplace will serve as the foundation for future expansion.

## 10. Technology and Product Principles

Everneed's development direction prioritises:

- Mobile-first design.
- Accessibility on affordable Android devices.
- Efficient performance on limited mobile data.
- Secure customer and seller accounts.
- Appropriate access controls and data protection.
- Reliable payment processing.
- Scalable platform architecture.
- Consistent user experience.

The platform is being developed through separate but connected customer-facing and web-based experiences.
